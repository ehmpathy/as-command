#### integration: the SIGTERM worker kill, diagnosed

- symptom: in 2 of 8 logged full runs (`01-47-31Z`, `01-48-18Z`; the other 6 green, 25/25: `01-48-39Z`, `01-49-11Z`, `01-57-27Z`, `01-59-12Z`, `01-59-39Z`, `02-05-36Z`) the jest worker that held `asCommand.integration.test.ts` was *"terminated by another process: signal=SIGTERM"* before it reported; the other 3 suites passed
- the suite alone: 4 of 4 green, 20/20 each (`--scope path://src/asCommand.integration`)
- cause: the host, not the code. `earlyoom` runs here (`/usr/bin/earlyoom`); it SIGTERMs the largest process when free memory crosses its floor. the box holds ~4.5k processes from peer sessions, ~6 GB of 32 GB available, load ~9. the kill lands only under 4 parallel jest workers
- no test spawns, signals, or kills a process except `scheduleFileLog.integration.test.ts`'s `spawnSync` of its own child, which cannot signal a peer worker
- so no repo change: a worker cap in the practice-owned jest config would quiet a host's memory pressure, not a defect (`rule.forbid.workarounds`); ci runners do not share this host
