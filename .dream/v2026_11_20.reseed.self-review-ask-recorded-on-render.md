# 🌙 dream: a self-review ask is recorded when its guide is rendered

- kind: reseed
- owner: bhrain (role=driver, `route.stone.set`)
- caught 2026-11-20

## .the cue that fired

on route `v2026_11_20.fix-publish-1-1-0`, a promise of review N renders review N+1's full guide with `passage = progressed`. a promise of N+1 right after then refuses `🍃 no ask on record`, and asks for `rhx route.stone.set --stone <s> --as passed` first. any edit to a stone's yield also rewinds the ask. so each of 13 self-reviews needed a hand-run re-ask, then a fresh write, then the promise.

## .the gap, precisely

the render shows the guide as if it were asked, but records no ask. the driver learns the true order (ask → write → promise) only by refusal. that is friction on the correct path (`rule.forbid.workarounds`: correct use meets no friction).

## .the shape of the fix

when `--as promised` progresses and renders review N+1's guide, record the ask for N+1 at that render, as `--as passed` would. the freshness bar then measures from the render, which is the moment the driver first reads the guide. keep the rewind on yield edits; it is correct.

## .why it is not done in this round

the skill lives in the bhrain package, not this repo.
